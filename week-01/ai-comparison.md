# Week 01 AI Assistant Comparison

## Common Question

What is the capital city of Australia?

---

## Tool 1

**Name:** ChatGPT

**Answer Summary**

ChatGPT answered that the capital city of Australia is Canberra. It also explained that many people confuse Sydney with the capital because it is the largest city.

**Strengths**

- Easy to understand
- Clear explanation

**Weaknesses**

- Information still needs verification.

---

## Tool 2

**Name:** Google Gemini

**Answer Summary**

Gemini also answered that Canberra is the capital city of Australia. It explained that Canberra was selected as a compromise between Sydney and Melbourne.

**Strengths**

- More historical information
- Clear explanation

**Weaknesses**

- Information still needs verification.

---

## Verification Source

Wikipedia

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canberra

---

## Final Comparison

### Accuracy

Both AI tools gave the correct answer.

### Traceability

Both answers needed verification using a trusted source.

### Explanation Quality

Gemini provided a little more background information, while ChatGPT gave a simpler explanation.

### Ease of Verification

Very easy because the answer could be confirmed using Wikipedia.

### Which claims required correction?

No corrections were required.

---

## Lesson Learned

Even when two AI models provide the same answer, I should verify the information using a reliable source before accepting it.
