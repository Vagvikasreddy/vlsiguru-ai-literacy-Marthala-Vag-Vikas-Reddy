# Mission 5 - Can AI Be Confidently Wrong?

## Objective

The objective of this mission is to understand that AI can give confident answers, but those answers should always be verified before trusting them. This helps build the habit of checking important information using reliable sources.

## Prompt

**What is the capital city of Australia?**

## AI Answer

The AI answered that the capital city of Australia is **Canberra**. It also explained that many people think Sydney is the capital because it is the largest city, but Canberra was chosen as the capital as a compromise between Sydney and Melbourne.

## Verification

I verified the answer using the official Australian Government website.

**Verification Source:**
https://www.australia.gov.au/

The official website confirmed that **Canberra is the capital city of Australia.**

## Result

**Result:** Correct

The AI gave the correct answer, and the official government website confirmed it.

## What My Test Proved

This test showed that AI can provide accurate answers for factual questions when the information is well known and easy to verify.

## What My Test Did Not Prove

This test does not prove that AI is always correct. Even if AI gives the correct answer in one case, it can still make mistakes or provide outdated or unsupported information in other situations. That is why important information should always be verified using reliable sources.

## Lesson Learned

This activity taught me that AI is a useful learning tool, but it should not be treated as the final source of truth. Even when an answer sounds correct, I should verify it using an official or reliable source before accepting it.
