## What is the most useful thing you learned this week?

The most useful thing I learned this week is that Artificial Intelligence is much broader than I originally thought. I understood the relationship between AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Generative AI, and AI Agents. I also learned that AI should be used as a learning assistant, not as the final source of truth.

---

## What did you initially misunderstand?

At first, I thought every smart application was Artificial Intelligence and that ChatGPT searched the internet every time I asked a question. After completing the missions, I understood that not every automated system is AI, and Large Language Models generate responses by predicting the next token instead of searching the internet for every answer.

---

## What did AI help you do?

AI helped me understand difficult concepts in simple language. It gave examples, explained the relationship between different AI technologies, helped me understand how Large Language Models work, and showed me how AI can be used in Design Verification.

---

## Where did you need to verify or correct AI?

I verified AI-generated information using Google's Machine Learning Crash Course, OpenAI Documentation, IBM AI documentation, and the official Australian Government website. This helped me understand that even when AI gives confident answers, important information should always be verified using reliable sources.

I want to understand how Large Language Models are trained on very large datasets and how GPUs help speed up the training process.
