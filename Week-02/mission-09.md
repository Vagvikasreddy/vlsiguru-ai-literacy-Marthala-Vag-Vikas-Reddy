# Mission 9 - Build My Own AI Working Agreement

## Objective

The objective of this mission is to create a set of personal rules for using Artificial Intelligence responsibly while learning engineering concepts. These rules will help me use AI effectively without depending on it blindly.

## My AI Working Agreement

### Rule 1 – Use AI to Learn, Not to Copy

I will use AI to understand concepts, learn new topics, and get explanations, but I will write my final understanding in my own words instead of copying AI responses.

### Rule 2 – Always Verify Important Information

If the information is important, I will verify it using reliable sources such as official documentation, textbooks, or trusted websites before accepting it. This rule is based on what I learned in Mission 4 and Mission 5, where I understood that AI can sometimes give confident but incorrect answers.

### Rule 3 – Never Share Confidential Information

I will never upload confidential company information, proprietary source code, customer data, passwords, or any private documents into public AI tools.

### Rule 4 – Take Responsibility for My Final Work

Even if I use AI for learning or getting ideas, I am responsible for checking the final answer and making sure it is correct before submitting or using it.

### Rule 5 – Keep Learning Beyond AI

I will not depend completely on AI. I will continue learning concepts, practicing problems, and improving my own understanding so that I can solve problems independently.

## Conclusion

This mission helped me understand that AI is a powerful learning assistant, but it should be used responsibly. By following these rules, I can use AI effectively while still developing my own engineering knowledge and taking responsibility for my work.
