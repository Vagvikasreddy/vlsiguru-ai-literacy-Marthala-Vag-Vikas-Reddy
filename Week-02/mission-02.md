# Mission 2 - AI, ML, Deep Learning and Generative AI

## Objective

The objective of this mission is to understand the relationship between Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI. These terms are often used interchangeably, but they are different concepts that are closely related.

## Answer

Artificial intellect (AI) is the general term for developing computer systems that can do tasks that usually need human intellect . These tasks include comprehending language , recognizing images , solving problems , and making decisions . Machine Learning (ML) is a subset of AI in which computers discover patterns from data rather than only perform preset rules specified by programmers. Deep Learning (DL) is a type of Machine Learning which employs artificial neural networks with multiple layers to learn complicated patterns from massive volumes of data. Generative AI is a type of AI that can create new content such as text, graphics, code, music, or movies by learning patterns from training data.

All of these technologies are linked together. AI is the largest field . Machine Learning refers to a method that can be used to generate artificial intelligence systems . Deep Learning is a subset of Machine Learning . Generative AI employs Deep Learning models to create new material . Through this relationship, I have come to understand that they are not two independent technologies, but rather various layers in the same area.
