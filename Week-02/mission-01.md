# Mission 1 - Find AI Around You

## Objective

The objective of this mission is to identify AI systems that I use in my daily life and understand what type of task they perform. This helped me realize that AI is already a part of many applications I use every day.

## Answer

Artificial Intelligence is already used in many programs we use every day but not every simple-looking software is genuinely AI. Some applications employ machine learning or deep learning to learn patterns from data. Others utilize generative AI to create content. In an effort to understand where AI is employed, I looked at a few applications I usually use and determined what kind of task they perform.

Google Maps employs machine learning and artificial intelligence to determine the optimal route and calculate the journey time by studying the traffic and historical data. Gmail utilizes ML to learn from past emails to determine if an email is spam or not spam. ChatGPT employs Generative AI to create text, respond to questions and assist users with a variety of tasks.

For Gmail Spam Filter, a simple rule-based application might filter emails containing terms like "free money" or "click here". However, since spammers can readily alter these terms, rigid regulations are not sufficient. Machine Learning does a better job since it learns patterns from a lot of spam and non spam emails instead of just predefined criteria.

## AI Around Me

| Application | AI Used | Task |
|-------------|---------|------|
| Google Maps | Yes | Prediction |
| Gmail Spam Filter | Yes | Classification |
| ChatGPT | Yes | Generation |

## Evidence

I checked Google's documentation about Gmail Spam Filter and Google Maps. I also referred to OpenAI documentation for ChatGPT.

## Conclusion

This activity helped me understand that AI is already present in many applications that I use every day. It also taught me that not every smart feature is AI, and it is important to verify whether AI or Machine Learning is actually being used.
