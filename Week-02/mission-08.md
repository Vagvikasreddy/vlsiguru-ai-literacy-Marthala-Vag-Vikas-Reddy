# Mission 8 - Where Could AI Help in My VLSI Track?

## Objective

The objective of this mission is to understand how Artificial Intelligence can be applied in Design Verification (DV) and why human knowledge is still important even when AI is used.

## Answer

My VLSI track is **Design Verification (DV)**. One important task in Design Verification is finding bugs, analyzing simulation results, debugging failures, and checking whether the design behaves according to the specification. Verification engineers also spend a lot of time reading logs, analyzing waveforms, writing test cases, and understanding why a test failed.

Artificial Intelligence can help by analyzing simulation logs, identifying failure patterns, generating testbench code, suggesting possible fixes, summarizing error reports, and automating repetitive debugging tasks. This can save a lot of engineering time and improve productivity. However, AI cannot replace a verification engineer because it does not fully understand the design specifications or the project requirements. The engineer must verify every AI suggestion, understand the root cause of the bug, and make the final decision before accepting any solution.

## Example

| VLSI Track | Task | How AI Can Help | Why Human Knowledge Is Still Needed |
|------------|------|-----------------|-------------------------------------|
| Design Verification (DV) | Debugging failed simulations | AI can analyze simulation logs, summarize errors, suggest possible causes, and help generate test cases. | The verification engineer must understand the design specification, verify the AI's suggestions, and decide the correct fix. |

## Conclusion

This mission helped me understand that AI can make Design Verification faster by helping with debugging, log analysis, and automation. However, AI is only an assistant, and the verification engineer is responsible for checking the results and making the final engineering decision.
