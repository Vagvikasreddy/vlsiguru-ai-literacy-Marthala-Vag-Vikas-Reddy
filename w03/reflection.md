# Week 03 Reflection

## What I learned

- ChatGPT was most useful for interactive explanations and debugging questions.
- Gemini provided a concise comparison and useful Google learning resources.
- Claude helped review longer technical explanations.
- NotebookLM was useful when answers needed to stay grounded in supplied sources.
- Perplexity helped discover sources, but citations still needed to be opened and checked.
- Verilator supports fast open-source RTL simulation and linting, but its language support, testbench setup and waveform configuration must be understood.
